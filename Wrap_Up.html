Capstone Wrap-Up Assignment

Final Submission Requirements

Tasks

  • Prepare your final capstone project for submission based on the type of project you completed:
    • D&D Projects: Turn your final product (e.g., Ed Tech Tool or Course materials) into a ZIP file containing all relevant artifacts (e.g., source code, lesson plans, assessments, slides).
    • Teacher Inquiry Projects: Compile your final inquiry artifacts, including data analysis, findings, and supporting materials, into a ZIP file.
  • Convert your final presentation into a PDF format and include all slides in the same order as presented.
  • Write a final reflection (500-700 words) discussing:
    • Your overall learning experience during the capstone project.
    • Key challenges faced and how you addressed them.
    • Insights gained about your field and professional growth.
    • Next steps for your project.
    • Next steps for your career and/or academic advancement.

Submission Instructions

  • Combine the following into a single ZIP file:
    • Final project artifacts (e.g., D&D product files or inquiry materials).
    • PDF version of your final presentation.
    • Final reflection document (in Word or PDF format).
  • Submit the ZIP file to the designated submission link in this course shell.
  • Ensure your submission is completed by the final deadline.
