Sprint 5: Prepare Final Presentation

Ed Tech Tool D&D: Final Presentation

Tasks

  • Develop a comprehensive presentation showcasing your Ed Tech Tool project.
  • Include key project phases: problem definition, ideation, prototyping, MVP development, and usability testing.
  • Demonstrate the functionality of your MVP, highlighting core features and their impact on stakeholders.
  • Reflect on challenges faced, solutions implemented, and lessons learned throughout the project.

Presentation and Slides Design Requirements

  • Design slides that are visually appealing, consistent, and professional.
  • Ensure the presentation is structured to fit within the 15-minute time frame.
  • Incorporate visuals such as charts, graphs, screenshots, or videos to support your points.
  • Practice the presentation to ensure clarity and adherence to time limits.

Deliverables

  • A Presentation Deck including:
    • Slides summarizing each project phase.
    • Visuals such as screenshots, diagrams, or demo videos of the MVP.
    • Key insights from stakeholder feedback and usability testing.
    • Reflections on the project journey and its broader implications.
  • A live demonstration of the MVP during the final defense presentation.

Course D&D: Final Presentation

Tasks

  • Prepare a detailed presentation summarizing your Course D&D project.
  • Highlight major milestones: problem definition, structural planning, module prototyping, and lesson implementation.
  • Present the fully implemented lessons, including key activities, assessments, and instructional strategies.
  • Reflect on feedback from pilot tests, revisions made, and lessons learned during development.

Deliverables

  • A Presentation Deck including:
    • Slides summarizing the course development process.
    • Sample lesson materials and visuals from implemented lessons.
    • Key feedback from pilot testing and resulting improvements.
    • Reflections on project outcomes and implications for teaching practice.
  • A live walkthrough of the implemented lessons during the final defense presentation.

Teacher Inquiry Projects: Final Presentation

Tasks

  • Create a presentation detailing your Teacher Inquiry project journey.
  • Include all phases: formulating the wonder, planning, data collection, and analysis.
  • Summarize key findings, patterns, or themes identified in the data analysis.
  • Discuss implications of your findings for educational practice and future inquiry.

Deliverables

  • A Presentation Deck including:
    • Slides detailing each phase of the inquiry project.
    • Charts, graphs, or visualizations summarizing data and findings.
    • Key reflections on the inquiry process and future directions.
  • A summary of findings presented during the final defense.
