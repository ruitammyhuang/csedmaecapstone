Overall requirements Due Friday (before meeting)

Prepare a 15-minute presentation that summarizes your entire project journey and outcomes.

Requirements

These expectations apply to all project types.

  • Prepare a 15-minute presentation
    Summarize your entire project journey, highlighting key phases and outcomes.
  • Set the project context clearly
    Include your project type, project goal(s), target learners, educational/application context, project scope, and why the project matters.
  • Include project-type specific details
    Use the section for your project type below to ensure you cover required elements.
  • Design professional, consistent slides
    Incorporate relevant visuals such as charts, graphs, screenshots, or videos where appropriate.
  • Reflect and synthesize
    Discuss challenges, insights, and broader implications of your work.
  • Practice for clarity and timing
    Rehearse to stay within the 15-minute limit and maintain clear delivery.

Ed Tech Tool D&D

▾

Demonstrate your fully functional MVP, highlight core features, and explain how your tool impacts stakeholders.

Specific deliverables

Include these elements in your presentation.

  • Live demo
    Provide a live demonstration of the tool during the presentation.
  • Core features and impact
    Highlight the MVP’s core features and how they address stakeholder needs.
  • Testing insights and refinements
    Share key insights from usability testing and explain final refinements made based on feedback.

Course D&D

▾

Showcase your full course structure and one fully implemented lesson, including evidence from pilot testing and improvements.

Specific deliverables

Include these elements in your presentation.

  • Course map and structure
    Showcase the entire course structure (course map).
  • One fully implemented lesson
    Show one fully implemented lesson and explain how it fits the course design.
  • Lesson overview
    Include an overview of the lesson’s objectives, activities, and assessments.
  • Testing feedback and improvements
    Share feedback from pilot testing and the resulting improvements.
  • Reflection
    Reflect on the lesson development process and lessons learned.

Teacher Inquiry Project

▾

Present your key findings and implications, supported by clear evidence and visuals, and reflect on impact on practice.

Specific deliverables

Include these elements in your presentation.

  • Data collection and analysis summary
    Summarize your data collection and analysis processes.
  • Visualize findings
    Use visualizations or charts highlighting patterns and themes.
  • Implications and reflection
    Discuss implications and reflect on the inquiry process and its impact on educational practice.
