Start Thinking About Your Capstone Project

This week

Begin brainstorming potential topics or problems in CS Education related to your practical context that you’re passionate about addressing.

Consider questions like:
  • What challenges have you encountered or observed in CS Education?
  • Are there gaps in tools, resources, or strategies that you’d like to explore?
  • What are you most passionate about in CS Education?
  • What ideas excite you and align with your professional goals?
  • What tangible tools or artifacts might you design, develop, or use within the semester scope?

You do not need to have a solid project idea before our first synchronous meeting. We will talk more about it in our meetings, and you will refine your ideas in the coming weeks.
