Final Presentation and Wrap-Up

First and foremost, successfully DEFEND your capstone project by successfully delivering your final presentation.

Then, finalize, package, and submit your complete capstone work

This is the final step of the capstone. You will package your final artifacts, submit a shareable ZIP file link, and include your final presentation PDF and reflection.

Final Submission Requirements Due Friday (before meeting)

Complete the checklist below and submit one ZIP file link to the Google Spreadsheet.

Tasks

Prepare your final capstone project for submission based on the type of project you completed.

  • Package your final project artifacts
    D&D Projects: Turn your final product (Ed Tech Tool or Course materials) into a ZIP file containing all relevant artifacts (for example, source code, lesson plans, assessments, slides).
    Teacher Inquiry Projects: Compile your final inquiry artifacts, including data analysis, findings, and supporting materials, into a ZIP file.
  • Export your final presentation as a PDF
    Convert your final presentation into a PDF and keep all slides in the same order as presented.
  • Write a final reflection (500–700 words)
    Address the following prompts:
    • Your overall learning experience during the capstone project
    • Key challenges faced and how you addressed them
    • Insights gained about your field and professional growth
    • Next steps for your project
    • Next steps for your career and/or academic advancement
Submission Instructions

Combine everything into a single ZIP file and submit a shareable link.

  • Create one ZIP file that includes:
    • Final project artifacts (D&D product files or inquiry materials)
    • PDF version of your final presentation
    • Final reflection document (Word or PDF)
  • Upload the ZIP file somewhere you can share a link
    For example, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox. Make sure access is set so the instructor can download it.
  • Submit your shareable ZIP link
    Paste the shareable ZIP file link into this Google Spreadsheets file under the corresponding worksheet.

Ensure your submission is completed by the final deadline shown above.

This wrap-up marks the culmination of your capstone journey and the completion of your Computer Science MAE Program.
