Start Thinking About Your Capstone Project

Begin brainstorming potential topics or problems in CS Education related to your practical context that you’re passionate about addressing in this project.

Consider questions like:

  • What challenges have you encountered or observed in CS Education?
  • Are there gaps in tools, resources, or strategies that you’d like to explore?
  • What are you most passionate about in CS Education?
  • What ideas excite you and align with your professional goals?
  • What are the tangible tools or artifacts you may design & develop or use in this project within the scope of this semester?

You don’t need to have to have a solid project idea before our first synchronous meeting. We will talk more about it in our meetings, and you will refine your ideas in the coming weeks.
