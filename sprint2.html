Sprint 2: Ideation / Data Collection Plan

D&D Projects: Ideation

Tasks

  • Brainstorm at least three potential design ideas that address the problem identified in Phase 1.
  • Consider stakeholder needs, CS education principles, and feasibility when generating ideas.
  • Engage in informal discussions or consultations with stakeholders to refine ideas.
  • Select one design idea to move forward with, providing a rationale for the selection.
  • Create simple sketches or diagrams to visualize the selected idea.

Deliverables

  • A Design Idea Brief including:
    • Three initial design ideas with brief descriptions of how each addresses stakeholder needs and aligns with CS education principles.
    • A detailed explanation of the selected design idea, including rationale and alignment with the problem identified in Phase 1.
    • Sketches or diagrams of the selected idea (hand-drawn or digital).
  • Supporting documents:
    • Notes from brainstorming sessions or discussions with stakeholders.
    • References to resources or frameworks that inspired the ideation process.

Please combine all required deliverables into one Google doc (add links to supporting documents if applicable), and then submit a sharable link to this discussion board. Make sure your peers can leave comments in your document.

Teacher Inquiry Projects: Data Collection Plan

Tasks

  • Identify the types of data needed to explore your wonder effectively.
  • Select appropriate methods for data collection (e.g., surveys, interviews, observations, or artifacts).
  • Develop instruments or tools needed for data collection (e.g., survey questions, interview guides).
  • Create a timeline for data collection activities, ensuring feasibility within the sprint.
  • Address ethical considerations, including how you will anonymize and secure participant data.

Deliverables

  • A Data Collection Plan including:
    • A detailed description of the data to be collected and its relevance to your wonder.
    • An explanation of the methods chosen for data collection.
    • A timeline for data collection activities over the next sprint.
    • A description of ethical considerations and steps for data anonymization and security.
  • Supporting documents:
    • Drafts of any instruments or tools (e.g., surveys, interview guides).
    • References to resources or examples that informed your data collection plan.

Please combine all required deliverables into one Google doc (add links to supporting documents if applicable), and then submit a sharable link to this discussion board. Make sure your peers can leave comments in your document.

Peer Review Requirement

Peer Review Tasks

Review at least one peer’s submission (Design Idea Brief or Data Collection Plan) before our bi-weekly meeting.

  • Clarity and Relevance: Evaluate whether the design ideas or data collection plan aligns with the Phase 1 deliverable.
  • Creativity and Feasibility: Provide feedback on the creativity of design ideas or the feasibility of the data collection plan.
  • Connection to Stakeholders: Assess whether the submission demonstrates a clear connection to stakeholder needs.
  • Suggestions for Improvement: Offer actionable suggestions to strengthen the submission.
